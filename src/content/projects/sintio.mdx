## About

Founded in March 2023, Sintio helps small and medium enterprises navigate digital transformation. Services range from e-commerce platform selection and setup to full IT strategy alignment with business goals.

## What We Do

- **IT Strategy** — Designing technology roadmaps aligned with long-term business objectives.
- **E-Commerce Solutions** — End-to-end implementation across Shopify, WooCommerce, and custom stacks.
- **Digital Transformation** — Process re-engineering supported by data and automation.
- **Business Analysis** — BRD/FRD documentation, requirement gathering, and stakeholder management.
